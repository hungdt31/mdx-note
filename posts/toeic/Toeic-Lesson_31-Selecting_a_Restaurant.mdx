## Words to learn

- **appeal** n., the ability to attract
  - A restaurant with good food and reasonable prices has a lot of appeal.
  - The new restaurant soon lost its appeal due to one waiter's mistake. 
- **compromise** n., a settlement of differences; v., to settle differences
  - The couple made a compromise and ordered food to take out.
  - John doesn't like the food at the nearby restaurant, so I compromised by cooking at home.
- **daringly** adv., bravely
  - We daringly ordered the *raw squid*.
  - He daringly changes the components of menu fit to visitors taste from Tokyo.
- **delicious** adj., tasty; good to eat
  - This restaurant is famous for its delicious soups.
  - The fish stew wasn't as delicious as we had expected it to be.
- **familiar** adj., often encountered or seen; common
  - It's nice to see some familiar items on the menu.
  - The chef blends the familiar tastes with the unusual.
- **guide** n., one who leads, directs, or gives advice; a guidebook
  - The guide led our tour group to a small restaurant only known to the locals.
  - I don't know where to go, so why don't we consult the guide.
- **majority** n., the greater number or part
  - The majority of the group wanted to try the new Chinese restaurant.
  - Claude was in the majority, so he was very pleased with the decision.
- **mix** v., to combine or blend into one mass; n., a combination
  - The daring chef mixed two uncommon ingredients.
  - The mix of bright colors on the plate was very pleasing.
- **rely on** v., to have confidence in; to depend on
  - You can rely on that website to give accurate restaurant reviews.
  - I seldom rely on restaurant reviews when choosing a restaurant.
- **secure** v., to get possession of; to obtain
  - Despite the popularity of the restaurant, Max was able to secure reservations for this evening.
  - The hostess secured us another chair, so we could eat together.
- **subjective** adj., particular to a given person; highly personal; not objective
  - Food preferences are subjective and not everyone agrees on what tastes good.
  - The reviews on this website are highly subjective but fun to read.
- **suggestion** n., a proposal; advice
  - Can I make a suggestion about what to order?
  - We followed the waiter's suggestion and ordered one of the specials.
