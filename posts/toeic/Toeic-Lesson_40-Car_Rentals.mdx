## Words to learn

- **coincide** v., to happen at the same time
  - The wedding coincided with a long weekend, so it was a good time to rent a car and go for a drive.
  - Sean hoped that the days for the discount on car rentals would coincide with his vacation, but they didn't.
- **confusion**: n., a lack of clarity, order, or understanding
  - There was some confusion about which discount coupons applied to which car rental agency.
  - To avoid any confusion about renting the car, Yolanda asked her travel agent to make the arrangements on her behalf.
- **contact** v., to get in touch with
  - Manuel contacted at least a dozen car rental agencies to get the best deal.
  - Last night I was contacted by my travel agent who said he had found a better price on a car rental.
- **disappoint** v., to fail to satisfy the hope, desire, or expectation of
  - Leila was disappointed that no rental cars were available the weekend she wished to travel.
  - I hate to disappoint you, but I can't allow you to rent a car unless you have a major credit card.
- **guarantee** v., to promise
  - The rental agency guarantees that all their cars are in good working order.
  - By reserving a car ahead of time, you will be guaranteed the make and model of car that you want.
- **intend** v., to have in mind
  - I never intended to drive to Los Angeles until mu brother suggested we do it together.
  - Do you intend to return the car to this location or to another location?
- **license** n., the legal permission to do or own a specified thing
  - First, I'll need to see your driver's license and a major credit card.
  - You will need a license in order to run this business.
- **nervously** adv., in a distressed or uneasy manner
  - In the city, Lonnie started driving nervously, so I volunteered to drive that part of the trip.
  - I looked around nervously the entire time I was in the dark parking garage.
- **request** v., ask for something politely or formally
  - When Sonia reserved her rental car, she requested a luxury model.
  - Most rental agencies request that you return the car with a full tank of gasoline.
- **temp** v., to be inviting or attractive to 
  - I am tempted by the idea of driving across the country instead of flying.
  - Gina is tempted to rent the smaller car to save a few dollar.
- **thrill** n., the source or cause of excitement or emotion
  - The thought of renting a sports car gave John a thrill.
  - Just taking a vacation is thrill enough, even if we are driving instead of flying.
- **tier** n., a rank or class
  - The car rental company had a few tiers of cars, each one costing more than the previous tier.
  - If you are on a budget, I suggest you think about renting a car from our lowest tier.
